Support enterprise IT operations, network infrastructure,
application systems, and physical security infrastructure
within an organization of over 400 employees.

### Career Progression

- Jr. Network Administrator | October 2025 – Present
- IT Support Assistant | February 2025 – September 2025
- IT Intern | November 2024 – January 2025

### IT Infrastructure & Technical Support

- Troubleshoot hardware, software, peripherals, account
  access, and network connectivity issues.
- Administer user accounts, permissions, password resets,
  and multifactor authentication through Okta.
- Prioritize, troubleshoot, and document technical support
  requests using Jira and MaintainX.
- Configure and troubleshoot audiovisual equipment for
  meetings and organizational events.

### Networking & Infrastructure

- Install and configure wireless access points, network
  switches, and connected infrastructure.
- Assist with network connectivity troubleshooting,
  infrastructure maintenance, and server administration.
- Coordinate infrastructure installations with internal
  departments and maintenance personnel.

### Application Development & Integration

- Develop and administer internal business applications
  using HCL Domino Leap.
- Gather application requirements through stakeholder
  meetings and translate business needs into technical
  solutions.
- Develop a Node.js proxy using NGINX and OAuth 2.0
  to integrate HCL Domino Leap applications with Okta APIs.
- Maintain and troubleshoot existing applications and
  integrations.

### Physical Security & Surveillance

- Install and configure approximately 50 IP surveillance
  cameras across organizational facilities.
- Administer and troubleshoot Milestone XProtect video
  management systems.
- Configure Axis, Hanwha, and Samsung Wisenet cameras.
- Optimize camera configurations, surveillance coverage,
  motion detection, and video storage utilization.
- Assist with access control infrastructure and
  integration with physical security systems.

### Documentation, Leadership & Training

- Maintain technical documentation for infrastructure,
  applications, and surveillance systems.
- Coordinate technical requirements with organizational
  leadership and other departments.
- Train personnel to support and maintain surveillance
  infrastructure.
- Develop and deliver foundational IT and cybersecurity
  instruction through a vocational education program.
